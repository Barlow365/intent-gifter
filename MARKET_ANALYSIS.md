---

## Market Gaps

| Gap | Opportunity |
|-----|-------------|
| **Preference ambiguity** | No platform helps people express "hard to articulate" preferences |
| **Bad gifts** | 30-40% of gifts are re-gifted or unused |
| **Wishlist staleness** | Traditional wishlists go stale, no dynamic learning |
| **Risk tolerance** | No platform lets users set category boundaries |

**Intent Gifter Solution:** Preference resolution via comparative choices + progress system.

---

## Target Market

### Primary Segments

**1. Gift Recipients (18-45)**
- Addressable: ~150M adults in US
- "Hard to shop for" subset: ~30M
- Serviceable: ~5M (early adopters)

**2. Thoughtful Gifters**
- People who care about giving meaningful gifts
- Overlap with recipients (same users, dual role)

### Secondary Segments

**3. Brands (B2B)**
- CPG brands wanting demand insights
- Fashion/apparel brands seeking engagement
- Target: 100-500 brands (Year 1-3)

## Competitive Landscape

| Competitor | Strengths | Weaknesses |
|------------|-----------|------------|
| **Amazon Wishlist** | Large user base, easy to add items | Static, no preference learning |
| **The Knot / Zola** | Wedding registries, group gifting | Lifecycle-specific, not general |
| **Giftster** | Family wishlists | No preference resolution |
| **Traditional Wishlists** | Simple | Stale, no learning, no boundaries |

**Intent Gifter Advantage:** Only platform with preference resolution + progress system.

## Go-to-Market Strategy

### Phase 1: MVP Launch
- **Target:** Early adopters via Product Hunt, Reddit

### Phase 2: Brand Partnerships
- **Target:** Fashion, food, home goods brands

## Competitive Advantages

1. **Data Moat:** Preference data from comparative choices
2. **Progress Trust:** Users trust system because progress never lost
3. **Legal Clarity:** Not gambling = regulatory advantage
4. **Network Effects:** Friend gifting drives organic growth

| Risk | Mitigation |

| **User churn** | Focus on retention, progress never lost |
